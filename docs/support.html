Include your app version, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, iCloud credentials, or private trip recordings. If a screenshot would help, hide names and sensitive locations first.

Recording a drive

Use Start Drive before driving and End Drive after parking. Allow location access when prompted. Trips contains your saved recordings and event reviews. Miles Safer records location only during an active drive.

CarPlay and family sharing

Both are optional. Open Settings → Setup guides inside Miles Safer for the step-by-step CarPlay and sharing guides. CarPlay shortcuts must be installed and their automations enabled in Apple's Shortcuts app. Sharing uses iCloud invitations; accepted completed reports appear in Family on the viewer's iPhone.

Missing speed limits

Open Settings → Offline maps to download road data for your area. Availability varies by region. Without a downloaded package, route and speed recording still work, but speed-limit checks are unavailable. Downloading maps later can recheck retained local trips.

OpenStreetMap limits may be missing or incorrect, particularly on smaller neighborhood roads. Road matching can also be uncertain. Always follow posted signs. The in-app speed-limit guide explains coverage and contributing corrections to OSM.

Managing trip history

Local recordings and received-report history default to two weeks, with an adjustable retention setting. Uploaded reports are managed separately through the sharing deletion control. For details about storage, sharing, and deletion, read the privacy policy.
