Privacy

Updated: November 10, 2026

About this policy

Drive Probe is developed by James Willis. This policy explains the current iPhone app, including optional family sharing. You can record and review trips without inviting anyone. Last updated: November 10, 2026.

Information recorded on your iPhone

During an active recording, Drive Probe saves precise location, route, speed, location accuracy, direction, timestamps and matched road/speed-limit information. It uses these records to create trip reports and possible speeding, acceleration and braking events.

With motion recording enabled, local diagnostic logs include acceleration, gravity, rotation and phone orientation. Logs also include iOS version, device model, battery/charging and low-power status, thermal status, app state, audio-output type (including CarPlay), permission status, lock/unlock-related system signals, errors, recording settings and any test markers you enter. These help explain events and investigate recording or battery issues.

Drive Probe does not read messages, contacts, browser history or the contents of other apps. Audio-output detection does not record microphone audio. Unlock-related events are inferred from iOS system signals; the app cannot see every interaction or determine which app someone used.

Location and other permissions

Location is used while a drive is recording, including in the background or with the phone locked. Always and Precise Location enable reliable background recording and optional CarPlay shortcut starts. Location recording stops when the drive ends. CarPlay disconnection normally ends recording after a 60-second grace period; an automation can fail, so check recording status while parked.

You choose whether to use motion recording, notifications and optional CarPlay automations. Manage permissions in iPhone Settings. End an active drive in the app and disable its Shortcuts automations if you no longer want automatic starts. Revoking permission does not delete previously saved trips.

Optional family sharing through iCloud

If you enable automatic sharing, existing retained and future completed trip reports are uploaded to your private Drive Probe iCloud collection using Apple CloudKit. Reports include your chosen driver name, precise route points, speeds, road/speed-limit matches, trip times, distance and event types/timestamps. Raw motion samples and device diagnostic logs are not included in shared reports. This shares completed reports, rather than a live location feed.

Only people you invite and who accept the invitation can read the shared collection through the app. A viewer downloads reports to their own phone. Sharing uses your Apple iCloud identity; Drive Probe does not create a separate login or ask for your Apple Account password. Apple processes iCloud account and service information under its own terms.

Turning off automatic sharing stops future uploads on this iPhone. It does not delete existing uploaded reports or revoke an invitation. Use the system sharing sheet, opened through Invite family member, to manage participants or stop sharing. Removed viewers may retain copies they already downloaded or exported.

Maps and network requests

Road matching and speed-limit checks use downloaded OpenStreetMap data on the phone. The app checks a public catalog and downloads map files from GitHub. These download requests do not include your recorded route or coordinates; the hosting service receives ordinary request information such as IP address and requested files/regions. Automatic map updates can make these requests in the background.

Trip-review maps use Apple MapKit. Apple may receive map requests and related map-area, location or network information as described in its privacy information. Opening OpenStreetMap or other external links sends you to that service, which has its own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

By default, completed local trips and downloaded family reports are kept for two weeks on each device. In Settings → Trip history, choose one week, two weeks, 30 days, 90 days or Forever. The limit is based on the trip end; interrupted recordings use their latest available timestamp. Cleanup runs when the app runs, rather than guaranteeing deletion at an exact time. Each device has its own setting.

This retention setting does not delete reports you previously uploaded to iCloud. To delete those reports, use Settings → Family sharing → Delete my uploaded reports. This turns off automatic sharing on this iPhone and deletes your uploaded reports from the current iCloud collection; local recordings and invitations remain. If deletion fails or is interrupted, retry it in the same screen. Sharing stays paused until deletion completes. If you deliberately re-enable sharing, retained local trips can upload again. Turn off sharing on any other recording devices before deleting.

Use Trip history to shorten local retention. To remove all local app data immediately, use iOS Delete App (offloading keeps its data). Removing local data does not delete uploaded reports. You can remove downloaded map packages in Offline maps. Deletion and revoked sharing cannot remove another person’s exported or previously downloaded copies, or copies retained in device/cloud backups under the relevant backup service’s retention practices. Removing the app does not by itself delete uploaded reports.

There is no separate Drive Probe account to close. Delete uploaded reports and stop sharing before removing the app if you want to remove the app’s cloud reports.

Developer access, exports and tracking

Drive Probe does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not sell trip data or use it for advertising. The app does not send raw trip logs to a developer-operated server. Optional shared reports are stored in the user’s CloudKit collection.

If you use an export or share sheet to send a recording, report or diagnostic information, the destination you choose receives that information. Exported logs can reveal precise locations and driving details. Review the destination carefully. Information you voluntarily send for support is used to respond and troubleshoot. Apple’s optional device diagnostics and TestFlight feedback are governed by Apple’s services.

Drivers and family consent

Drive Probe is intended for drivers and families reviewing driving habits. It is not designed for children under 13. Explain recording and sharing to the person carrying the phone and obtain any consent required for your use. Family members should agree on who may view trips and how long copies will be kept.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact James Willis at jrmwillis@jrmwillisconsulting.com. Please avoid sending precise routes or other sensitive trip details unless they are needed for your request.

This policy may change as the app changes. The current version and update date are available in Settings → Legal & Privacy and on the public policy page.
