OpenStreetMap road and speed-limit data

Road geometry, road names and speed-limit data: © OpenStreetMap contributors. These data are available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.

Drive Probe’s offline road databases are adapted from OpenStreetMap extracts, including extracts provided by Geofabrik. Adaptations select drivable roads, normalize supported speed tags and package road geometry/attributes into regional grid databases. The adapted databases are distributed under ODbL 1.0, with notices and source/snapshot details in each map package.

The public map-data repository and its release downloads provide the adapted databases, build scripts and license notices. ODbL applies to the map databases; it does not place your personal trip recordings under an open-data license.

Apple map display

The trip-review basemap is supplied by Apple MapKit and has its own attribution. OpenStreetMap supplies offline road and speed-limit matching data, rather than that basemap. Apple Maps branding and legal links remain available on the map.
